myHaul

In the app

  1. Open myHaul and go to your profile.
  2. Tap the menu (☰), then Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Finish any open orders first, because the money is still being held.

Without the app

Email help@myhaul.co.za from the email on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your name, bio, posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, follows, chats and devices are deleted straight away. Order and payment records are kept for 5 years because the law requires it, then deleted.
