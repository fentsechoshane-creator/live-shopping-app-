Who we are

myHaul is run by [company or owner name] [CONFIRM], Cape Town, South Africa. Contact us at help@myhaul.co.za. Our Information Officer under POPIA can be reached at the same address.

What we collect

  • Your email or cell number, name, bio and profile details.
  • For sellers: ID document and selfie (for checks), bank details (held by our escrow partner), and shop details.
  • Your posts, photos, videos, comments, likes, reposts, follows and messages.
  • Orders, delivery details and payments. Card details are handled by the payment provider, not us.
  • Device and usage data to keep the app working and safe, and a notification token if you allow notifications.

Why we use it

  • To run your account, show you posts, and process orders, deliveries, returns and payouts.
  • To keep the app safe: checking sellers, stopping fakes and scams, and handling reports.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax and financial records.

Who we share it with

  • Our escrow and payment partner (TradeSafe), couriers you choose, video hosting (Mux), our database host (Supabase), and Apple or Google to deliver notifications.
  • We never sell your information to advertisers.

Your rights

  • See, correct or delete your information. Delete your account in the app (Menu, Delete my account) or see Delete your account.
  • Complain to us, or to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. After you delete it, we keep order and payment records for 5 years as the law requires, then delete them. Photos, videos, messages and comments are deleted straight away.

Community rules

  • No fakes or replicas, no hate or harassment, no nudity, no scams.
  • Keep payments in the app. Paying outside removes buyer protection.
  • Report anything that breaks these rules. Our team checks reports within 24 hours and may remove content or close accounts.

Buying and selling

  • Payments are held by our escrow partner until the return window closes, then paid to the seller.
  • Buy now items: 7 day change of mind returns from delivery (you pay return postage). Faulty items: up to 6 months.
  • Sellers pay a 6% fee per sale. Founding sellers pay 0% for 90 days.
